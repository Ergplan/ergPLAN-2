# Stage 10 — Monthly state rule books

Run on 21 Nov 2026, from `stage-10-start` (the commit tagged `stage-9-done`).

## What was built

- **The rule book** (`report/statebook.py`): for a DISCOM and a month, for a reference industrial consumer or one client's connection — the grid tariff per time-of-day slot; the landed cost of open-access solar and wind (in-state and inter-state) for three routes (third party; captive with 26% equity, i.e. group captive; a captive plant) and of IEX day-ahead, green day-ahead and real-time power; the saving on the grid tariff; how open-access energy is settled after banking, in sentences citing the approved settlement rule; how an exchange purchase is settled; limits; the rule versions used; what is not sourced. The cost lines are `engine.landed_cost`'s, through the same function as the landed-cost screen (`platform.routers.landed.landed`).
- **Issues and review**: `state_rulebooks` (migration 0013, audit trigger). A book is a frozen content with its SHA-256: draft → approved or rejected (a note is required to reject); approving supersedes the consumer's earlier issue for the month; a draft lists every figure that differs from the last approved issue. Clients see approved books only.
- **The month in one action**: `POST /statebooks/generate-month` — a reference book per DISCOM with open-access rules (DISCOMs of a state with identical figures share one book) and a book per client connection. Pending drafts and unchanged approved books are left alone.
- **The file**: `GET /statebooks/{id}/file`, Markdown, every figure footnoted; a page footnote links to the stored order page.
- **Every number opens its source** (the person's instruction during the stage): in the review screen each figure is a control that opens its sources — the order or regulation page (stored PDF at that page, with the quoted words and the regulator's link), the State Regulation Master's citation, or the assumption. Each book counts how many of its figures open a stored page, how many are cited by name only, and how many are assumed (`content.trace`), on screen and in the file.
- **Captive exemption**: the cross-subsidy and additional surcharge rows of every state carry the exemption for captive and group-captive consumption (statute, `register` refs); cited versions without it were re-issued (Maharashtra v3, Karnataka v2, Punjab v2, Telangana v2, NPCL v5; the register states' v2).
- **Web**: `/statebooks` (by state; generate the month; one book with your own reference consumer and quoted PPA tariffs) and `/statebooks/{id}` (review, approve, reject, download).

## What was skipped, and why

- A scheduler for the first of the month and a notice that drafts await review: the month is generated by an analyst's action (known gap).
- PDF export of a book from the app (the file is Markdown); the combined PDF given to the person was made outside the app.
- A client-facing page for the approved book: the API serves it (`/connections/{id}/statebook`); the plan page does not link to it yet.

## Deviations from the master prompt

- `standby_charge` is no longer a landed-cost line (Stage 4 listed it): it is the rate for standby supply, and Chhattisgarh's ₹11.93/kWh had been added to every open-access unit. Decision of 21 Nov 2026.
- The rule book states the banking charge beside the landed cost (the landed-cost screen keeps it as an upper bound inside).
- Filling a cited state's missing open-access components from the State Regulation Master was built and withdrawn within the stage (rule 13): Maharashtra's wheeling charge and losses stay "not yet sourced".
